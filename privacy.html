Daily Routine

Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

Daily Routine is private and local-first. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated account system. Your routines, reflections, notes, medication logs, and backups are not sent to the developer.

Information you create

Routine entries, check-ins, notes, memories, prayer entries, settings, and other information you enter are stored locally on your device. In the web app, this information is stored in that browser’s local storage. In the native iPhone app, it is stored within the app’s on-device web storage.

Apple Health

In the native iPhone app, Apple Health access is optional and requested only after you choose Connect Health. If you grant permission, Daily Routine reads the categories you approve from:

  • Step count
  • Sleep analysis
  • Workouts

The resulting summary is displayed on your device. Daily Routine does not write to HealthKit, use health information for advertising or analytics, send it to the developer, or include it in Watch complications. You can deliberately include the latest summary in a manual accountability report; it is off by default, shown in the report preview, and shared only to the destination you choose.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps exchange today’s routine progress and user-initiated quick actions through Apple’s Watch Connectivity framework. The Watch app and its complication store only a small on-device routine snapshot in an App Group shared by those Daily Routine components. They do not receive Apple Health data.

Reports, backups, and exports

Accountability reports, backups, and CSV exports are created only when you request them. A report stays on your device until you choose Copy or Share, and the app shows the exact report text first. You choose where reports and files are sent or saved. Once copied, shared, or exported, their privacy depends on the destination and service you select.

External links and hosting

Daily Routine can open websites, Shortcuts, Maps, calls, messages, email, and other links that you configure. Those services have their own privacy practices. When you use the web version, GitHub Pages may process ordinary web request information under GitHub’s privacy terms; Daily Routine does not receive that information or add tracking scripts.

Retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, clear the browser’s storage, or uninstall the native app. Use Setup → Backup & export → Reset app data to remove Daily Routine’s local records. Exported backup files must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if Daily Routine’s data practices change. For help or privacy questions, visit the Daily Routine support page.
