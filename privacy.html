Daily Routine

Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026

Daily Routine is private and local-first. The app has no advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking. Private sync is optional and begins only after you connect an account.

Information you create

Routine entries, check-ins, notes, memories, prayer entries, settings, truth-reminder text, reminder pictures, and other information you enter are stored locally on your device. In the web app, routine information is stored in that browser’s local storage. In the native iPhone app, routine information is stored within the app’s on-device web storage, while the separate truth-reminder library is stored in the app’s private local files.

Optional private sync

If you connect Private sync, the app uses Supabase for email authentication and owner-only cloud storage. Supabase receives your sign-in email address and a synchronized copy of routine definitions, daily entries, notes, memories, weekly reviews, and ordinary app preferences. Database security policies restrict the app to the row belonging to the signed-in account.

Apple Health summaries, HealthKit authorization, automatic Health-based step values, Earned Access baselines and progress, truth reminders and their pictures, background photos, downloaded-backup timestamps, local snapshots, and conflict-recovery archives are excluded from the synchronized document. The Supabase project administrator may have technical access needed to operate or troubleshoot the service, but synced information is not used for advertising, analytics, or sale.

Apple Health

In the native iPhone app, Apple Health access is optional and requested only after you choose Connect Health. If you grant permission, Daily Routine reads the categories you approve from:

  • Step count
  • Sleep analysis
  • Workouts

The resulting summary is displayed on your device. If you add the automatic steps routine item, its daily value is filled from Health on that device and excluded from Private sync. Earned Access can record the step count when you start a round, calculate only the additional steps walked, and hold the earned-time status on that device; this information is not synchronized or shared automatically. Sleep-derived bedtime and wake-time suggestions do not change routine history until you review and confirm selected times; confirmed times become ordinary routine history and may be included in Private sync. Daily Routine does not write to HealthKit, use health information for advertising or analytics, send it to the developer, or include it in Watch complications. You can deliberately include the latest summary in a manual accountability report; it is off by default, shown in the report preview, and shared only to the destination you choose.

Truth reminders and notifications

The native iPhone app can schedule local notifications from text and pictures you add to its truth-reminder library. The library remains on that iPhone and is excluded from Private sync and routine backups. Reminder content may appear on the Lock Screen according to your iPhone notification settings. Daily Routine does not transmit the reminder library to the developer.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps exchange today’s routine progress and user-initiated quick actions through Apple’s Watch Connectivity framework. The Watch app and its complication store only a small on-device routine snapshot in an App Group shared by those Daily Routine components. They do not receive Apple Health data.

Reports, backups, and exports

Accountability reports, backups, and CSV exports are created only when you request them. A report stays on your device until you choose Copy or Share, and the app shows the exact report text first. You choose where reports and files are sent or saved. Once copied, shared, or exported, their privacy depends on the destination and service you select.

External links and hosting

Daily Routine can open websites, Shortcuts, Maps, calls, messages, email, and other links that you configure. Those services have their own privacy practices. When you use the web version, GitHub Pages may process ordinary web request information under GitHub’s privacy terms. When you enable Private sync, Supabase processes authentication and database requests under its privacy terms. Daily Routine does not add advertising or analytics scripts.

Retention and deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, clear the browser’s storage, or uninstall the native app. Use Setup → Backup & export → Reset app data to remove local records. Disconnecting Private sync removes the sign-in session from that device but does not by itself delete the cloud copy. Use Setup → Private sync → Delete cloud copy and disconnect to remove the synchronized routine document while keeping the local copy on that device. The closed owner account can be removed administratively on request. Exported backup files must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if Daily Routine’s data practices change. For help or privacy questions, visit the Daily Routine support page.
