Help for version 1.9.2

Back up before troubleshooting. In Daily Routine, open Setup → Backup & export → Download backup. Your routine information is local to your device, so a backup is the safest recovery path.

My data or settings are missing

Make sure you opened the same installed web app, browser profile, or native app you previously used. Data does not automatically move between separate browsers or installations. If you have a JSON backup, use Restore backup in Setup.

Apple Health is not updating

  • Open Daily Routine → Setup → Health & Watch.
  • Choose Connect Health and approve only the categories you want to share.
  • Use Refresh summary after permission is granted.
  • You can review or revoke access in the iPhone Health app at any time.

Watch quick actions are locked

Complete today’s Truth Before Tasks opening on the iPhone. The Watch unlocks its quick actions after the iPhone sends the completed state. Medication logging remains intentionally available only on iPhone.

The Watch is not syncing

Open Daily Routine on both iPhone and Apple Watch, keep them nearby, and confirm Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are enabled. If the Watch is temporarily unavailable, supported quick actions queue and deliver after the connection returns.

Report a technical problem

Use the public issue tracker for reproducible technical problems. Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not include private routine entries, prayer notes, medication details, health information, backup files, passwords, or verification codes.
